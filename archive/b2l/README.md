## 🎯 The Core Edge: Exploiting Longshot Place Inefficiencies

In UK/Irish handicap racing, public and market money disproportionately clusters around favorites and low-odds runners. At the long end of the market (**odds of 20.0 to 100.0+**), market pricing becomes significantly less efficient.

Standard bookmaker place terms (**1/4 or 1/5 of odds for top 3, 4, or 5 places**) pay out massively on outsiders:
- A **33/1 (34.0)** runner placing at 1/4 odds pays **8.25/1 (9.25)** on the place part.
- A **50/1 (51.0)** runner placing at 1/5 odds pays **10.0/1 (11.0)** on the place part.

By filtering outsiders using **strict Last Time Out (LTO) pace, sectional stride decay, and in-running speed comments**, the B2L system isolates live, competitive outsiders with an **11.5% to 18.1% place strike rate** — turning longshot betting into a highly profitable, low-drawdown strategy.

## 🧠 The Scoring Model (Zero Lookahead)

All features are calculated **strictly from the horse's prior completed race (LTO)**:

### ➕ Positive Signals
| Signal | Points | Form Rationale |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Front Runner / Prominent** (`L`, `P`, `F`, `LEAD`) | **+3** | Dictates race tempo, stays out of trouble, ideal for B2L / Place |
| **Positive Speed Keyword** | **+2** | In-running comment shows strong finish (*"ran on", "quickened", "headway"*) |
| **Claiming Jockey Edge** | **+2** | Weight allowance gives significant handicap relief |
| **Quick Return (DSLR ≤ 7 days)** | **+2** | Trainer capitalizing on peak fitness |
| **Clean Discipline** | **+2** | No bad jumping or behavioral vices in LTO |

### ➖ Negative Penalties
| Signal | Points | Form Rationale |
|---|:---:|---|
| **High Stride Decay (ASL − SL_Finish ≥ 0.20m)** | **-3** | Physically tied up/decelerating in final furlong |
| **Bad Discipline Keyword** | **-2** | Swerved, hung, dwelt, reared, or lost ground |
| **Long Absence (DSLR > 120 days)** | **-1** | Ring-rusty after extended layoff |

## 💡 Practical Execution & Pro Tips

1. **Target Extra Place Races Daily**:
   - Focus on races where bookmakers (Bet365, SkyBet, PaddyPower, Betfred, William Hill) offer **Extra Places** (e.g., 5 places on 16+ runners or 4 places on 8–15 runners).
2. **Best Odds Guaranteed (BOG) Advantage**:
   - Take early prices in the morning. If the horse drifts out from 25/1 to 40/1 at post time, BOG guarantees you get the higher 40/1 payout.
3. **Account Longevity & Distribution**:
   - Rotate your daily bets across **5 to 8 bookmakers** so no single account draws excessive extra-place extraction flags.
4. **Betfair Exchange Place Market Fallback**:
   - If bookmaker accounts face restrictions, place bets directly in the **Betfair Exchange Place Market** (Top 3 or Top 4).
